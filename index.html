startmyclaim.ai — AI-Native Legal Tech

AI Start-up

Built solo in 8 weeks  ·  0 developers  ·  0 legal staff  ·  Full-stack AI operations

I built startmyclaim.ai to answer a specific question: can a single person, working with advanced AI, create and operate a fully functional business from scratch — without a team? The answer, proven in eight weeks, is yes.

The platform enables individuals to pursue consumer compensation claims and is live, growing and fully operational. It was built without writing a single line of code, without legal qualifications and without any external resource. AI was deployed across every layer of the operation — product, legal training, website, SEO, paid advertising, content and social — replacing what would typically require a team of five to eight specialists.

This was a deliberate experiment to push the boundaries of what AI makes possible when combined with senior strategic expertise. Everything is automated. Content is AI-generated and continuously optimised. The business runs.

The approach I developed to build startmyclaim.ai is something I now offer directly to clients: a structured, AI-accelerated capability for building new business functions, products or digital ventures at a fraction of traditional cost and time.
